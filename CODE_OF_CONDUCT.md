# Code of conduct

This project is a place to make WordPress sites safer. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, the support forum or anywhere else the project is represented) is expected to help keep it welcoming and useful.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to security or to WordPress. Someone whose site was just hacked is having a bad day.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you assume.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic, and credit other people's work.
- Protect privacy: don't post anyone's site details, credentials, IP addresses or personal information without their permission.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Sexual language or imagery
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Posting working exploits for unpatched problems in public, instead of reporting them privately ([SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md))
- Repeatedly derailing discussions, or pressuring people to do unpaid work

## Enforcement

Report problems to **info@jeremyanderson.tech**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code of conduct, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who does so, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
